RahRow is now centered on one product direction: a cross-platform,
privacy-first V2Ray client platform.

## What this means

- Runtime modules and protocol adapters are now first-class workstreams.
- Shared platform packages are being shaped for long-term maintainability.
- Product and developer documentation are being rewritten around the V2Ray
	client direction.

## Current status

RahRow remains pre-alpha. The monorepo is still in foundation mode, and many
packages are scaffold-level placeholders while architecture and contracts
stabilize.

This update marks a scope correction so future releases represent the actual
RahRow product: a modern, cross-platform V2Ray client platform.
