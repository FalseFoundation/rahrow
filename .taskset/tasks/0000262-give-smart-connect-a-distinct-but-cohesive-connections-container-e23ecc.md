Restyle Smart Connect to match the full RahRow visual and interaction system while giving its container a restrained, intentional distinction comparable to the Home power-control container. Reuse semantic tokens and shared primitives; do not create a second design language or hard-code feature colors.

Preserve progress, cancellation, error, disabled, winner, scheduled, light/dark, RTL, reduced-motion, narrow mobile, and desktop states. The distinction must remain clear without relying on color alone.
